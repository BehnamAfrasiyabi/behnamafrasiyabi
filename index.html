Hello, I'm Behnam Afrasiyabi

< />

I have always been interested in the design and development of embedded systems for various applications. During my university education, I built a system for managing home appliances using a cell phone with a smart home approach. After that, I designed a device for test and simulation of in-vehicle sensors, and I have been involved in a project to build a car tester and ECU diagnostics.

After graduation, I started my career on the production floor — manufacturing and soldering industrial boards by hand. That hands-on experience gave me a deep understanding of the real-world challenges that come with volume production. Today, when I design a board, I design it to be manufactured — factoring in DFM, testability, and assembly from the start, so the transition from prototype to production is smooth and problem-free.

Every board I deliver is designed to meet EMC standards, optimized for signal integrity, and ready for volume production — not just a prototype that works in the lab.
